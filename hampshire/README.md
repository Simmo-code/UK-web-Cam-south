# Hampshire Webcam Operations Map

**Checked:** 28 July 2026

## Contents

- `index.html`: interactive single-file map
- `hampshire_webcams_full_register.csv`: full sortable register
- `hampshire_webcams.geojson`: GIS-compatible point data
- `hampshire_webcams_google_mymaps.kml`: import into Google My Maps / Google Earth

## Scope

- Ceremonial Hampshire, including Portsmouth and Southampton.
- Isle of Wight and West Sussex cameras are excluded, even when they look into Hampshire.
- Publicly accessible live video, refreshing images, weather/aviation cameras, wildlife cameras and public traffic-camera viewers are included.
- Three official portal/location-map entries are included separately.
- Public-safety CCTV is not described as publicly viewable unless a public image exists.

## Position accuracy

Traffic-camera operators do not provide a convenient unrestricted coordinate export. Where exact coordinates were available from the camera archive, they are identified as **Published exact coordinates**. Other motorway pins have been placed inside the labelled junction section and are marked **Road-section estimate**. They should not be treated as surveyed camera-mast positions.

## Use

Open `index.html` in a modern browser with internet access. The camera data itself is embedded in the file; internet access is needed for the basemap and camera websites.

Use the All / Webcams / Traffic / Flying-weather buttons, search, category filters and quick area buttons. Click a pin or list row, then choose **Open camera / viewer**.

## Maintenance

Webcams are frequently moved, removed, renamed or temporarily disabled. The CSV is the master register for future updates.
